Our Story

A landmark in King Harbor since 1976

Captain Kidd's is a fish market and restaurant located in beautiful King Harbor in Redondo Beach. With its breathtaking views and extensive seafood menu, it's sure to provide a great dining experience.

Opened in 1976 by Harry and John Mills, Captain Kidd's has become a landmark seafood market and restaurant in Redondo Beach — a local gem where locals and tourists alike come to indulge in the freshest seafood from around the world.
